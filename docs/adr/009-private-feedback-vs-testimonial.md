# ADR 009 — Private feedback vs public testimonial

## Status

Accepted (Phase 2A-M)

## Context

Doctor feedback improves the product; marketing testimonials are a separate legal/privacy act.

## Decision

- Private feedback is accepted whether positive or negative.
- Public testimonials require **explicit consent**, doctor selection of publishable text, no patient information, manual approval, withdrawable consent, and audited publication status.
- Positive feedback is **never** auto-published.
- Moderation must not reject negative feedback solely for sentiment.

## Consequences

Typed contracts and deterministic moderation foundation ship in Phase 2A-M. Transmission remains NOT_CONNECTED.
